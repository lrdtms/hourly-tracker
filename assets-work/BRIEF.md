# Brief: Hourly Tracker brand assets

**Product:** Android app that asks "what did you do?" on the clock and charts where the day went.
**Audience:** one person (the owner), glancing at it many times a day.
**Feeling:** calm, precise, a little editorial. A quiet instrument, not a game. Matches the in-app look
(Instrument Serif greeting, tonal panels, one deep teal accent).
**Anti-goals:** a generic stopwatch/clock icon; gradients, glows, glass; text baked into images; anything loud.
**Assets wanted:** app icon (options to choose from), animated splash (options to choose from).

## Assumptions (the owner delegated the direction; these are my calls, not theirs)
- Mark ideas come from the product itself: check-in slots, the hour boundary, time split by activity.
- Flat colour only, from the app's palette. Brand teal `#1B6E66`; ink `#1F201D`; paper `#F6F5F1`; one warm pop `#EB6834` (the app's chart orange).
- Android only (adaptive icon: separate background + foreground, mark inside the central 66/108 safe zone).
- Splash = native static splash (Android cannot animate it) followed by an in-app animated GIF page.
  Every animation starts and ends on the resting mark, so the hand-off from the native splash and the loop are seamless.
- No wordmark inside the GIF: the app name is real text laid under it.
